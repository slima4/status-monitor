A good incident update reduces uncertainty

An incident update is a short, timestamped message that explains the customer impact, what is known, what the response team is doing, and when customers will hear more. It should report confirmed facts without hiding uncertainty or inventing a resolution estimate. For the full method, read the guide on how to write incident status updates.

01

Impact first

Say what customers experience before describing internal systems.

02

Facts, not guesses

Separate what you have confirmed from what is still being investigated.

03

Promise the next update

Give a specific update time and post again even if the situation has not changed.
